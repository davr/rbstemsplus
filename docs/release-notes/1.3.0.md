RB Stems Plus 1.3: prepare a playlist's stems before a set.

## What's new

- **Prepare playlists.** The new **Prepare** tab lists rekordbox's playlists. Choose one and RB
  Stems Plus says how many tracks are ready, how many it will prepare, how much space that takes
  and about how long. **Prepare** makes their stems in advance, in the background, so every
  track loads with its stems at once in rekordbox, as if you had loaded it before. **Pause** and
  **Stop** are there and in the Prepare menu; what is done stays done. Needs Stems Cache and a
  Mac with Apple silicon.
- **Kept until a date you choose** (10 days by default): Stems Cache doesn't remove prepared
  stems meanwhile, unless you lower its size limit below them (on a drive shared with another account or Mac, its limits apply too). Up to 5 playlists, using at most 75% of Stems Cache's size limit, so there's
  room left for the tracks you load during the set. A playlist that doesn't fit can be prepared
  in part.
- **Checked when rekordbox opens.** The **Status** tab lists your prepared playlists and whether
  each one is still ready. When you open rekordbox, RB Stems Plus checks them too and tells you
  with a notification if a track isn't fully prepared any more, or was added since: click
  **Prepare** again for those. The files of tracks on another drive or in your Documents, Desktop,
  Downloads or a cloud folder, and stems saved on another drive, aren't looked at then, because
  macOS would ask over rekordbox: look at the Status tab before a set.
- **A new window:** three tabs, **Status**, **Prepare** and **Manage** (installing, reinstalling
  and uninstalling each feature). **Uninstall RB Stems Plus…** is in the RB Stems Plus menu.
- **Reinstalling after a rekordbox update:** RB Stems Plus now asks once rekordbox's updater has
  finished and its window is closed, not while it is still running.

## Updating

RB Stems Plus offers the update when you open it (**RB Stems Plus › Update RB Stems Plus…**).
Once updated, RB Stems Plus asks to update Stems Cache too (**Update**, with your password, or
**Not Now**): preparing playlists needs the new Stems Cache. As with every update, macOS asks you
to allow RB Stems Plus again when it updates Stems Cache.

When you prepare a playlist with tracks in your Documents, Desktop or Downloads folder or on an
external drive, macOS asks once whether RB Stems Plus may read them. Click **Allow**: without it
those tracks are skipped, and the list says why. macOS also asks whether RB Stems Plus may send
notifications, the first time it has something to tell you, and the same for RB Stems Plus Watcher:
the check when rekordbox opens can only tell you if RB Stems Plus Watcher is allowed.

rekordbox must be set to *Prioritize sound quality* for STEMS (Preferences › Extensions › STEMS):
prepared stems aren't used with *Prioritize speed*.

## Install

Paste this in Terminal and press Return:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/Gabe-LS/rbstemsplus/releases/latest/download/bootstrap.sh | bash
```

The app opens and walks you through the rest. Installing Stems Cache takes about a minute.

## What you need

- A Mac. Tested on macOS 26 Tahoe on an Apple Silicon Mac. Preparing playlists needs Apple
  silicon.
- rekordbox 7.2.17, 7.2.18 or 7.2.19, with STEMS working (turned on at least once).
- About 1.5 GB of free space, and an administrator account for Stems Cache. Prepared stems take
  about 13 to 16 MB per minute of music, depending on the model (AAC files up to twice that).
